**Regen Coordination** aims to catalyze the growth and sustainability of the regenerative finance (ReFi) ecosystem, particularly within the context of Web3 technologies. Our focus is to empower local communities and ecosystems to thrive with blockchain-based tools that help to drive social, ecological, and economic impact.

## **North Star Outcomes**

To effectively assess the progress toward our goals, Regen Coordination has defined three **North Star outcomes** that guide and align our efforts:

### **🌟 Increase Awareness, Engagement, Adoption, and Development of ReFi Web3**

The goal here is to build recognition, involvement, and integration of **ReFi Web3** tools and systems across a broad spectrum of users, builders, and projects. ReFi Web3 refers to the leveraging of blockchain technology and decentralized financial solutions to promote ecological, social and economic regeneration.

- **Awareness**: Refers to how well people know about ReFi Web3. This includes raising the visibility of ReFi Web3 philosophies, projects, protocols, and their benefits within global and local communities. The more people understand the potential of ReFi Web3, the more likely they are to engage with it.
- **Engagement**: Engagement refers to how actively people are participating within the ReFi Web3 ecosystem. This could include interacting with ReFi Web3 apps and tools, contributing to discussions, or taking part in decision-making processes within decentralized organizations (DAOs).
- **Adoption**: This outcome focuses on how many people and projects are using ReFi Web3 apps as well as integrating them into their local communities, projects, and ecosystems. It’s about ReFi Web3 becoming a **core tool** for local regenerative initiatives, and a part of mainstream use for various economic activities.
- **Development**: Development in this context refers to the increase in the number of **builders**, **developers**, **founders**, and **innovators** creating new ReFi Web3 tools and systems, as well as adapting them to local needs and potentials. This outcome is key to ensuring that ReFi Web3 scales and evolves over time, leading to sustainable growth.

### **🌟 Increase Awareness, Adoption, Development, and Financial Activity on Celo, Ethereum & the Wider Ethereum-Aligned Ecosystem**

Celo, Ethereum, and the wider Ethereum-Aligned ecosystems (e.g. other L2s) form the technological backbone of ReFi Web3, as well as being a key source of funding and grants. Their health and growth are integral to the success of the entire ecosystem. Our goal is to promote and sustain the growth of these blockchains by increasing **awareness**, **adoption**, **development**, and **financial activity** across the ecosystem.

- **Awareness**: Increasing awareness means more people know about Celo, Ethereum, and other blockchain projects. This could involve outreach campaigns, educational events, or partnerships that bring attention to the technical, financial, and social benefits of using these blockchains for regeneration.
- **Adoption**: Adoption here focuses on the growth of actual users and projects that build on Celo, Ethereum, and related networks. This includes the utilisation of decentralized applications (dApps), the integration of blockchain into existing systems, and the migration of traditional finance or social systems onto these platforms.
- **Development**: Development refers to the **growth of projects** and **builders** who contribute to the technical and ecosystem development of Celo, Ethereum, and their associated layer-2 networks. This could involve creating new decentralized applications, customising solutions, and enhancing user-friendliness.
- **Financial Activity**: Financial activity tracks how much capital flows through the ecosystem, particularly within regenerative finance. Key metrics include **Total Value Locked (TVL)**, **Total Value Flowed (TVF)**, **transaction volume**, **active users**, **etc**. The focus here is on sustainable economic activity—ensuring that capital flows are used to create long-term value in the ecosystem, not just temporary speculative investments.
    - Rather than Web3 grant capital being immediately offramped, with communities never engaging with Web3 again (which leads to poor Web3 ROI), Regen Coordination aims to pilot and support key mechanisms and strategies to ensure that local communities **onboard to Web3** and **retain value flows within Web3 rails**. This approach creates sustainable local economies where Web3 tools are not just a one-time funding solution, but an integrated part of the local ecosystem that drives long-term economic vitality.
    - **Total Value Flowed (TVF)** reflects the dynamic, ongoing movement of capital within blockchain community networks, providing a more holistic view of economic activity and long-term sustainability. TVF can track the amount of value moving through local Web3 payment networks, public goods funding rounds, community credit pools, and local stablecoins that stay within the local economy (on Web3 rails), rather than being extracted or quickly offramped to traditional financial systems.

### **🌟 Create & Catalyse Local Ecological, Social, and Economic Impact**

- **Create**: The core team or community leads direct action to implement ecological, social, and economic change. For example, this might involve projects like regenerative agriculture, renewable energy initiatives, or community-driven wealth-building projects that use Web3 technologies.
- **Catalyse**: In this scenario, the core team or community acts as a **catalyst**—empowering other local actors, projects, or organizations to take action and create impact. For example, this might include providing funding, technical assistance, or networking support to other regenerative projects.
- **Ecological, Social, and Economic Impact**:
    - **Ecological Impact** refers to actions that regenerate and restore ecosystems—whether by protecting biodiversity, improving carbon sequestration, or creating new nature-based solutions.
    - **Social Impact** involves empowering local communities, improving quality of life, and addressing social inequities.
    - **Economic Impact** includes the creation of wealth, economic resilience and the strengthening of local economies and opportunity.

## Regen Coordination 🤝 Ethereum Localism

[Ethereum Localism](https://greenpill.network/pdf/ethereum-localism.pdf) aims to embed Ethereum-based funding, governance and applications into real-world local economies, using tools like quadratic funding, local currencies, and decentralized finance to create tangible impacts and expand Ethereum’s utility beyond digital spaces. Regen Coordination aims to scale proven pilot programs to bridge Ethereum infrastructure with grassroots initiatives, ensuring funds remain in local economies and measurable impacts are recorded on-chain.

Rather than Web3 grant capital being immediately offramped, with communities never engaging with Web3 again (which leads to poor Web3 ROI), Ethereum Localism and Regen Coordination aim to pilot and support key mechanisms and strategies to ensure that local communities onboard to Web3 and retain value flows within Web3 rails. This approach creates sustainable local economies where Web3 tools are not just a one-time funding solution, but an integrated part of the local ecosystem that drives long-term economic vitality.
