| **Suitability of the partner organisation (technical, administrative and management competences)\*** | \[Textbox, max. 1500 character including spaces\] Gaia has collaborated with Indigenous governments in the Colombian Amazon to advance two interconnected objectives: strengthening Indigenous territorial governance and revitalizing cultural systems. Through an intercultural and rights-based approach, Gaia has helped secure legal protection for over 25M hectares of Indigenous territories, enhancing governance institutions and supporting the recognition of Indigenous Territorial Governments (ETIs). Last year, ETIs were formally recognized by presidential decree, granting them legal standing equivalent to municipalities. Gaia acknowledges the autonomy of Indigenous governments and has signed agreements allowing ETIs to manage funds directly while building their financial and technical capacity. Over the past five years, Gaia has managed US$9.7M in donor funding to lead conservation initiatives and support Indigenous governance.
