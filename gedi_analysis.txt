GEDI Level 2 products (L2A/L2B) provide discontinuous LiDAR tracks rather than spatially continuous rasters, meaning they lack coverage for the vast majority of our high-resolution training grain coordinates. While there are gridded products (L3/L4), their coarse spatial resolution (1km) is misaligned with our strict-core feature extraction. Due to this fundamental structural limitation and the resulting massive rate of missing data at the strict-core pixel level, we will continue to EXCLUDE GEDI from the merged V4.7 table.
